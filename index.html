Why this lesson matters

The baskets are empty and everyone is full. One last conversation: paying the bill, and maybe saving a little money while you're at it.

🎯 Interaction purpose

The meal is finished. You ask for the bill, choose a payment method, and check whether a credit-card promotion is available. Then, at a family meal, you join the friendly fight over who pays.

✅ By the end you can…

  • Ask for the bill: 唔該，埋單呀 m4 goi1, maai4 daan1 aa3
  • Understand 想畀卡定畀現金呀？ soeng2 bei2 kaa1 ding6 bei2 jin6 gam1 aa3?
  • Choose 信用卡 seon3 jung6 kaa1 or 八達通 baat3 daat6 tung1
  • Ask about a promotion: 有冇信用卡有優惠呀？
  • Offer to pay: 今餐我請啦！ gam1 caan1 ngo5 ceng2 laa1
Completion goal: ask for the bill, choose cash, credit card, or Octopus, and check politely for a credit-card promotion using clear Hong Kong Cantonese.

🎵 The six tones

The number at the end of each Jyutping syllable tells you the pitch. Tap a card to hear it. Same sound si, six different words.

💡 Tones 1 3 6 are flat; tones 2 5 are rising; tone 4 is falling.
🎧 Check your audio settings before recording tap to open
In every step you'll listen to the model, record yourself, then play yours back. You can't move on until each one is done, so set up first:
  1. Allow the microphone. When your browser asks, click Allow. If you clicked Block, use the 🔒 or ⓘ icon by the address bar to turn the microphone back on, then reload.
  2. Pick the right mic. Mac: System Settings → Sound → Input. Windows: Settings → System → Sound → Input. Make sure the level moves when you talk.
  3. Turn the volume up and use headphones if you can, so the playback doesn't echo.
  4. Get a Cantonese voice. The model audio uses your device's Cantonese (Hong Kong) voice. Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices → Chinese (Hong Kong). Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices → Chinese (Traditional, Hong Kong SAR). Chrome or Safari work best. The 🗣️ Real voice buttons play a real recording and work everywhere.

🏷️ Decode the discount

Hong Kong discounts tell you what you pay, not what you save. 九折 gau2 zit3 means you pay 90%, so it's 10% off.

    🏮 Celia's tip: spend money wisely

    In Hong Kong shopping malls, restaurants sometimes join limited-time credit-card promotions. Discounts may apply only to selected cards, spending amounts, dates, or other terms. Ask politely before paying because the promotion may not be offered automatically. If you do not ask, you may miss it.

    爭住埋單 zang1 zyu6 maai4 daan1 means competing to pay the bill. It can be a playful part of some Hong Kong family meals. 今餐我請 gam1 caan1 ngo5 ceng2 means “This meal is on me.”
